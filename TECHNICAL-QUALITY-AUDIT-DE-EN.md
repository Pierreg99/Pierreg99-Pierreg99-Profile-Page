## 4. Quality vs Cost Model

For a **2026 DACH planning model**, published market guidance puts software-development rates roughly around **€90–€150/h for established agencies**, with DACH IT-freelancer medians around **€95–€105/h** depending on source and definition. One current benchmark places German full-stack development around **€75–€140/h**, with a €100/h median. These are external planning ranges, not a valuation of Pierreg99. citeturn701619search0turn701619search8

For the team comparison below, cost is normalized to a notional **€100/h blended rate** so that capability and delivery economics can be compared independently of a specific vendor quote.

| Model | Typical Team Composition | Relative Quality Potential | Cost Multiplier | Best Fit |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Solo Generalist | 1 developer | 6.5–8.0 | **1.0×** | MVPs, prototypes, focused tools |
| Senior Full-Stack | 1 senior developer | 8.0–9.0 | **1.2×** | Complex apps with concentrated ownership |
| 2-Person Expert Pod | Senior FS + specialist | 8.5–9.3 | **1.7×** | AI + web, 3D + web, product acceleration |
| 3-Person Full-Stack Team | Lead + FS + QA/FE | 8.7–9.5 | **2.5×** | Product delivery with review and QA separation |
| 5-Person Specialist Team | Lead + FE + BE + AI/Data + QA | 9.0–9.7 | **4.2×** | Larger production products |
| Enterprise Squad | 6–10+ incl. DevOps/PM/Security | 9.2–9.9 | **6×+** | Regulated/critical platforms, SLA-heavy systems |

### Value-for-cost interpretation

The strongest economic position of this portfolio is **breadth per contributor**: a single capable developer can cover areas that often require several specialists in a conventional team. That does **not** mean one person replaces a mature production organization. Review capacity, security, operations, UX research, product management and parallel delivery remain team advantages.
